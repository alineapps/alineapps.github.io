Clipboarder

Your clipboard history, with a double Ctrl press.

A Windows app that brings up your clipboard history when you press Ctrl twice.

  • History of text, images and files
  • Pastes into the previous app automatically
  • Pinning, favorites and search
  • Your history stays on this PC
